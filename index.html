❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & 3D Printing Guide... ▼

📡 What are Vertical Fine Artifacts (VFA) and how does the VFA Radar help?

VFA refers to repeating vertical ridges or ripples on outer walls caused by stepper motor resonance at specific speeds. LayerSpy's VFA Radar identifies outer wall speeds falling into standard resonance corridors (typically 45–65 mm/s and 90–110 mm/s) so you can optimize slicer speeds for glass-smooth surfaces.

🎯 What is Pressure Advance (Linear Advance) and Corner Bulging?

When a 3D printer decelerates into a sharp corner, melt-zone pressure builds up, causing excess plastic to ooze into swollen corners (Corner Bulging). Pressure Advance (Klipper) and Linear Advance (Marlin) dynamically decrease extruder pressure. LayerSpy audits corner deceleration points (Δv > 60 mm/s) and lets you embed calibrated values directly.

⚡ How does the real Klipper & Marlin kinematics simulation work?

Standard viewers simply divide line distance by nominal feedrate. LayerSpy uses a 2D trapezoidal motion planner with forward/backward passes, calculating exact print durations based on printer acceleration (M201/M204), jerk/square corner velocity (M205), and junction speeds.

🧵 What is Z-Seam Alignment and why should I check it?

The Z-seam is the start/stop point on every layer. LayerSpy's Z-Seam tool highlights all layer start points in 3D, letting you verify whether your slicer has hidden the seam in sharp inner corners or placed it away from visible aesthetic faces.

🔥 What is the Volumetric Flow Rate limit (mm³/s)?

Volumetric flow rate represents how many mm³ of filament your hotend can melt per second (Flow = Speed × Layer Height × Line Width). If speed demands exceed hotend capacity (e.g. >15 mm³/s on standard hotends), under-extrusion and extruder clicking occur. LayerSpy's flow heatmap highlights dangerous flow spikes.

🔒 Does LayerSpy upload my G-Code files or 3D models to any server?

No, absolutely zero files or 3D geometries leave your device. LayerSpy processes everything 100% locally in your browser using Web Workers and WebGL on your local GPU. It even works completely offline as an installable Progressive Web App (PWA).
